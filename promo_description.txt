Liberry is built for book lovers. Designed to make the world’s most important public domain literature accessible to everyone, Liberry is more than just a reader—it is a powerful tool for discovery, organization, and lifelong learning.

Explore tens of thousands of free books from public domain repositories, all available with zero download limits, fees, or quotas. Build a personal library that travels with you, annotated with a powerful interconnected note-taking system.

Liberry is a one-time purchase. No subscriptions, no ads, no recurring fees. Just you and the world’s greatest stories, forever.

KEY FEATURES

DISCOVER & EXPLORE
• Access 60,000+ free ebooks from public domain repositories.
• Take on "The Intro 100": A foundational challenge spanning from The Odyssey to The Great Gatsby.
• Explore "Global Stories" to read masterpieces from Africa, The Middle East, Asia, and Latin America.
• Dive into expertly curated thematic lists like "The Russian Soul", "Voices of the Veil", "Pulp, Weird, & Noir", and "Shadows & Spectres".
• Search instantly across repositories, even offline.
• Seamlessly download books directly to your library.

READING EXPERIENCE
• A beautiful, distraction-free reading environment.
• Customize your experience with multiple fonts, text sizes, and reading themes (Light, Dark, Sepia).
• Choose your preferred scroll mode: Vertical, Horizontal, or Two-Column Landscape.
• Smart progress tracking keeps your place across all your devices.

AUDIOBOOKS
• Integrated Audiobook Player: Listen to your favorite classics with a seamless, full-featured player.
• Smart Playback Controls: Adjust playback speed (0.5x to 4.0x), skip forward/back, and use the sleep timer.
• Miniplayer: Keep listening while you browse your library or organize your notes.
• Offline Listening: Download audiobooks for zero-data listening anywhere.
• Background Playback: Continue listening while using other apps or with your screen off.

ORGANIZE & ANNOTATE
• Build a connected database of your reading life.
• Highlights: Save favorite passages and link them to your thoughts.
• Journal: Write personal reflections linked to specific text.
• Characters: Track characters with custom bios and linked quotes.
• Bookmarks: Never lose a pivotal moment.
• Export your notes and highlights to CSV or TXT to own your data forever.

SHARE, SYNC & OWNERSHIP
• Send to Kindle: Seamlessly send any book to your Kindle reader.
• Export EPUBs: You own the files. Export or share them with any other app.
• Sync your library, reading progress, and notes across all your devices (iOS, iPadOS).
• Your library is yours: Offload books to save space without losing your metadata.
• Complete Data Freedom: You have power over your data and files, forever.
